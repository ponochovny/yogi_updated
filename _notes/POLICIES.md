## Cancellation Policy

- Standard packages (5 classes): If a client cancels well in advance (e.g., 12 hours in advance), the credit is automatically returned to the account. If they cancel at the last minute (Late Cancel), the credit is forfeited.

- Unlimited plans: To prevent people from booking everything "just in case," a no-show fee is introduced. If a client doesn't show up or cancels at the last minute, they are either charged a real fee (e.g., $10) or, after three such cancellations, are blocked from booking for a week in advance.
